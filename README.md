# Blinkit Sales Dashboard (DVA Project)

This project is a Data Visualization and Analysis project built using Google Sheets for data cleaning and Looker Studio for dashboard creation.
The objective of this project is to analyze Blinkit sales data and generate meaningful business insights using interactive visualizations.

# Project Overview

Blinkit is a quick commerce grocery delivery platform operating across multiple outlet locations. This project analyzes its sales dataset to understand sales performance, outlet distribution, product trends, and revenue patterns.
Workflow followed in this project:
Raw Dataset → Data Cleaning in Google Sheets → Dashboard in Looker Studio

# Tools Used

Google Sheets for data cleaning and preprocessing
Looker Studio for dashboard visualization
CSV dataset

# Brand Performance & Profitability

Net Profit Leadership: The platform achieved a total net profit of 51,476.88 with a healthy average selling price of 488.36.

Hero Product: Pet Treats emerged as the top profit generator (3,488.96), significantly outperforming other high-volume items like Lotion and Biscuits.

Inventory Health: Maintained an average shelf life of 231.76 days, ensuring low wastage across the 11 active categories.

# Voice of the Customer (VoC)

Service Bottlenecks: Delivery was identified as the most active feedback category, indicating it is the primary driver of customer sentiment.

Rating Analysis: While the average rating sits at 3.34, a large volume of "Neutral" feedback (approx. 34.8%) suggests a significant opportunity to convert average experiences into "Positive" ones.

# User Growth & Audience Segmentation

Revenue Concentration: The platform reached a milestone of 29M+ in Gross Revenue, fueled by a balanced mix of Regular, Premium, and New customers.

Regional Stars: Deoghar and Orai were identified as the top revenue-generating areas, providing a roadmap for localized expansion.

# Growth Marketing & Acquisition

Channel Efficiency: Email marketing proved to be the most efficient channel with the highest ROAS of 2.49, followed closely by SMS at 2.38.
   
Scale vs. Spend: Despite high spend on the App channel, conversion data suggests that Referral Programs and New User Discounts offer better revenue-to-spend scaling.

# Last-Minute Delivery & Fulfillment

Speed Benchmarks: Achieved an impressive average delivery time of 6.32 minutes for an average distance of 2.72km.

Delay Mitigation: Traffic was identified as the #1 reason for "Significantly Delayed" orders, suggesting a need for better route optimization during peak hours.
